## Overview

`internal/screen` turns the `.abbd/` store into the blueprint a human reads. `Load` reads the store, `Build` derives a `Model`, and `Files` renders the blueprint's files. The pages are static HTML with no script, cut from the one model: `index.html` holds only the overview, each module or feature that holds units or has a focus view on the overview has its own `<ID>.html`, each flow, stack and table has its own page, and `contracts.html` and `units.html` index every contract and every unit. `graph.json` holds the unit rows, every live relation, the module lists without their drawings and the figures, for tools. So a change rewrites the pages of the views that draw it, and a change inside one module rewrites that module's page and `graph.json`. The pages and `graph.json` cite a quote by its file and words through `cite`, without its lines, so moving a quote's lines alone rewrites no page. A change to the lines around a quote marks its claim STALE, and that mark lands on the pages that show the claim. The CLI's `publish` (`cmd/abbd/screen.go`) runs after every command marked `writes` (`setup`, `sync`, `record relation|unit|parent|fact|evidence`, `retract`, `migrate`, `understand`, `forget`, `flow`, `stack`, `table`). It renders nothing for an empty store. Once every file is written and any other file under `blueprint/` removed, it records in the state's `Blueprint` the inputs hash it rendered from and in `Rendered` the hash of the files it wrote, so no file holds any value of the sync. `abbd status` reuses `Load`, through the same `screenInput`, for its freshness verdicts, and the page points at `abbd status` instead of stating one. The package imports `internal/model`, `internal/store`, `internal/topology` and `internal/narrative`, never writes the topology, and draws no box the store lacks, apart from a focus view's twin.

The overview draws each top-level module and feature as a box, and a top-level contract no module provides as a loose box. A `contract` line runs from a user's chip on the bottom edge to the provider's chip on the top edge. One chip stands for the contracts that line carries, named when it is one and counted when it is more, and the contracts no line reaches fold into one `+N 계약` chip on the provider. A thick `bypass` line with an open head marks a module dependency no contract line already carries. Two modules that depend on each other that way share one `mutual` line with a head at each end. A relation from a unit at any depth is lifted to the top-level unit that contains it. INFERRED couplings are drawn too, because sync rechecks their quotes. Only dead ones are dropped, and a drifted one is drawn like a live one. A drawn edge counts as extracted when any relation under it is the script's, else stated, else inferred. A small circle at a line's start marks a reader's or a human's relation, a bundle of both is `mixed`, and a dashed line runs against the reading direction. Every top-level module and every unit that holds others has its own view, and a box links to one only when `viewOf` names it, so no link opens a missing page. A feature with no parts has none. Everything is sorted before it is placed, so the same store always draws the same picture. `docs/blueprint-svg.md` states the drawing rules. `legible_test.go` measures them per line and channel, and `trace_test.go` traces every relation along the drawn ink and counts the bends and the short jogs on its path.

## Key Concepts

- **`Input` / `Model`** (`screen.go`): `Input` is what `Load` reads, including each module's understanding and its code digest now. `Load` leaves `Tree`, the git snapshot, for the caller to fill. `Model` is what `Build` derives. It holds the unit catalogue, the `Overview`, `Links` between two contracts listed as text, one `Inside` per view, the overview's `Focus` views, every live relation as `[]Line` for `graph.json` (`graph.go`), and the extraction command the page footer names.
- **`tree`** (`tree.go`): the parent tree the views follow. A unit whose parent is absent moves to the top level, so nothing present drops out. A unit with children and no live relations of its own is abstract, and an inside box for it draws its members' relations (`sources`). `within` and `outside` say which child of a view, or which largest unit outside it, stands for an end.
- **`overview`** (`overview.go`): the plan `planOverview` builds and the insides reuse. `top` lists the top-level units and the live relations between them, with the stored relations and origins each drawn edge stands for. Each top-level contract gets its `providers` and `users`, and a module that provides a contract is not drawn as its user.
- **`Drawing` / `Box` / `Port` / `Wire`** (`overview.go`): one SVG picture with its coordinates set. A `Box` is a top-level module or feature, an inside unit, or a loose contract. Its `Href` opens a view the blueprint holds, and it stays empty when there is none. On the overview, `chipFor` makes one chip per set of contracts a drawn line carries, labelled with its one contract or with `계약 N개` joined by a no-break space so `shorten` keeps the word. A module whose chips need more than `chipWrap` wraps them into at most two rows instead of widening, and the last row folds what does not fit into one "+N" `Port`. `settle` then moves a single unfolded row of chips over the x their lines run at. A chip is at most `chipMax` wide: `chipLines` wraps a longer label onto more lines, broken after a separator or before a space, so it is never cut and never leaves its edge, and every chip in a row takes the row's height, which `chipBand` sums for a box or a frame edge. On an inside frame, two ports that land on one spot share a chip, and its `Keys` name every port it stands for. `Drawing.Relations` lists the pairs of places the view requested before routing, which the checker holds the ink to. `hover` numbers a drawing's boxes and chips, a twin with its unit, and classes each line and dot by the shapes at its ends and each shape by the shapes its relations join it to. The fixed `hoverCSS` rules then fade the rest while a reader points at one shape, with no script on the page. A drawing with more than `hoverMax` shapes takes no part, and only a page with a drawing carries the rules. A climbing line is drawn in a lighter tint of its colour, and `faint` lightens the dots only climbing lines meet at.
- **`arrange` / `layered`** (`layout.go`, `layered.go`): the layered layout. It orders nodes to leave few edges pointing back (Eades, Lin and Smyth), ranks without those edges, and sets each node above what it uses. A node nothing uses sits just above the highest thing it uses, and nodes with no line gather on a bottom layer. Each pair of places more than a row apart gets a `chain` of dummies. `order` sorts every row, chains included, by one `tactic`, then sets a box joined to nothing last in its row. `Build` lays each full view out with every tactic, and `choose` keeps a later tactic's drawing only where its `cost` on the routed ink beats the best so far, which starts as the first tactic's. Fewer touches beat outright. Each ordering is drawn twice: wide, where `place` lets every block spread over the whole budget toward what it joins and then trims the drawing to what the blocks cover, and `narrow`, packed as tight as the order lets it, at `narrowBudget` for a unit's view. The draw closure leaves each drawing's cost in the `read` it is handed, and a focus view, handed none, is never weighed. `wrap` splits a layer wider than the budget into rows. Where the boxes with a line to what lies below fit in a wrapped layer's last row, `stack` puts them there, so their lines leave from the bottom row. `shed` takes the shortest chains out while the rows are still too wide with every chain crooked. `place` treats each box and each straight run of a chain as one block, Brandes and Köpf style. Each chain, longest first, becomes as few runs as it can: a run grows down while the order holds and the drawing fits its budget, found by halving. `shorten` then moves an item along its row, past up to `moveMost` items, where that saves more than `crossCost` of line on the placed drawing, a crossing counted as `crossCost`, and the rows are placed again unless that takes the drawing past its budget. A focus view passes a `focusSeat`, so `seat` orders rows by the full view's x, `brick` deals a wide ring into interleaved rows, and `ring` packs each row around the focus on one vertical axis.
- **`route`** (`route.go`): orthogonal routing on that grid. A line crosses each gap on its own track and passes rows down its chain or between boxes. A line with no chain keeps the x it passed the row above at where the row lets it, and `gutterTo` steps it aside toward its end. A chain no line uses keeps no room, a passing line keeps off the `stubs` into the boxes beside its row, and the lines of a box's chips and of the box itself never share an x on one edge. Lines of one kind that leave one box share a departure trunk through every row two of them still pass. Two or more lines of one kind into one box edge share an arrival bus, unless they all ride one departure bus into different boxes, and boxes in one row share one only when exactly the same places reach them. Boxes that the same two or more places all reach may share one arrival bus instead, from `share`. `route` draws a view both ways and keeps those shared buses only when `weigh`, on the routed ink, finds no more touching ink of one kind between unrelated places, crossings or side-by-side runs, and fewer bends and jogs along each line's whole way. A view with more than `shareMost` requests is drawn plain, since `weigh` compares every pair of strands. `viaX` keeps a step off a departure track between the two trunks, and `stepX` finds a step onto an arrival track at the nearest clear x. Each bus is drawn once with its line count, and `place` sizes each gap with room for the counts.
- **`junctions` / `anchor`** (`ink.go`): what the routed paths look like to a reader. `junctions` puts a `Dot` wherever ink of one colour goes three or more ways, and where a line crosses onto a trunk of its own kind that runs past it. `anchor` sets each ×N beside its own stub or drop, on the side where the nearest other ink is farthest.
- **`mark` / `paint` / `gaps` / `labels`** (`marks.go`, `hues.go`): what the page adds to the routed ink after the dots and counts. `mark` records the place each line's relations leave, cuts the gaps and sets the labels. After every view is drawn, `Build` calls `colours` once over all of `Model.drawings`, so a unit wears one colour in every view. Two units are joined when some view draws a line between their boxes, a contract's chip included, and `colours` colours the most constrained unit first, picking the colour its joined units hold least. `paint` then gives each box its colour, drawn as a left accent bar and a tinted border, draws each line in the colour of the boxes its lines leave, or a neutral grey when they differ, and gives it an arrowhead whose shape says its kind. A direct dependency that skips the contract boundary wears the reserved warning orange instead, whatever box it leaves. `palette` is the one table of six colours, and `hueCSS` writes it, neutral and the warning into the page for both themes. `TestEveryHueReadsOnBothThemes` holds each at 3:1 or better, `TestTheWarningStandsApart` keeps every colour 30 or more from the warning under normal vision, protanopia and deuteranopia, and `TestJoinedBoxesWearDifferentColours` holds joined boxes to different colours. `gaps` cuts a horizontal piece wherever a vertical piece of another line crosses it away from both pieces' turns and ends, unless a dot of the kind both lines share joins them, as a `Gap` in an SVG mask on that line's path, whose id hashes only that line and its holes, so a change to one line renames no other mask. Crossings closer than `gapJoin` on one piece share one cut, so no sliver is left. The path keeps its points, so the checker's ink graph is unchanged. `labels` then sets the word for a line's `via` (호출, 데이터, ...) upright in 11px text beside one straight stretch of its own ink, horizontal stretches first, clear of boxes, chips, counts, dots, origin marks, arrowheads, other labels and other lines, when every relation the line stands for names that one `via`. A bus whose lines all say one word is labelled once. `TestEveryCrossingIsAGap` reads the rendered SVG and holds every crossing to a hole wide enough for the stroke over it, every hole to a crossing, and no sliver under 4px. `TestLabelsReadAlongTheirLine` holds each word upright, on the canvas, beside its own line and off everything else.
- **`Inside`** (`inside.go`): one view built by `(*overview).inside`, within the overview's 1320 pixel budget, or 920 for a narrow tactic. Its frame carries the module's contracts and dashed `← name` or `→ name` ports for units outside it met without one. Lines from outside units into inside units come in on top. A crowded side hands its least committed ports to the other side, and the frame never grows past the budget. It also holds `Units` with one sentence each, `Unwired` contracts kept off the frame and grouped by a first word that three or more share, `Inferred` couplings with an end in the module or a direct child, `Up` and `Trail`, and the `Understanding` of the module the view is for. Every top-level module and every unit that holds others has a view, so a nested module that holds units shows its text below its own inside. One that holds none has no view, and `Build` puts its text on its `InsideUnit` in the parent's view, where the page folds it under the unit's entry.
- **`Focus`** (`focus.go`): a view cut from a dense one, which has more than two boxes and more than twice as many relations as boxes. Each module or feature box of a dense overview gets one, and each box of a dense inside. `planOverview` or `inside` plans the full view and keeps only the lines with an end at the focus and the boxes at their other ends, so a focus view never draws a line the full view lacks. A unit that both uses the focus and is used by it is drawn a second time below as a twin, ID `<unit>↓`, and `base` maps it back. Focus views take the overview's width. The overview's sit on the page of their unit, before its inside, and an inside's on its module's page. A focus view of an inside names its boxes by their last path segment. Focus views link each ring box to its own focus view, and stay out of `graph.json`. `Pages` gives a copy of each full view to `linkFocus`, which makes each box that opens nothing else open its focus view. `centres` keeps the ring in the full view's order. `TestFocusViewsArePartialGraphsOfTheirView` and `TestFocusViewsKeepTheMapOfTheirView` hold both rules.
- **Freshness** (`screen.go`): `ActualFreshness` compares the working-tree snapshot with the synced fingerprint. `BlueprintFreshness` compares the state's `Blueprint`, the inputs hash the blueprint was rendered from, with the inputs hash now. It also compares the state's `Rendered` with `Input.Rendered`, the `store.BlueprintHash` of the files there now that `Load` reads, so a page missing, added, edited or left from an older render reads STALE, a module's own page included. Either is `UNKNOWN` when a side is missing, and either is `STALE` when the state is `Merged`, since a merged state's values match neither the merged tree nor its inputs. `Model` holds no freshness verdict and no value of the sync, so a render changes only with the data.
- **`Understanding`** (`words.go`): a how text cut into `Block`s of headings, list items, paragraphs, pipe tables and fenced code, and `Span`s of plain, `code` and bold text. `blocks` reads the text through `narrative.Lines`, so the page splits it at the headings the readability report counts, and a `#` or `|` line inside a fence stays code. A fenced block becomes one block of kind `pre`, whose `Code` holds its lines as written. A table's `Rows` start with its header, drop the rule line and keep an escaped `\|` in its cell. It is marked `Stale` when its stored digest differs from `store.ModuleDigest`, which hashes the files of the module and of every unit under it at any depth, and the lines around the quotes they cite in other files. The template draws it through `howhead` and `howtext` in both places, and `howtext` draws its blocks with the `blocks` template the notes use too. It escapes the text and never trusts its markup. A module with no text gets one line that says so and names the `abbd understand` command that stores one, and a unit with no responsibility line shows none.
- **`Note`** (`notes.go`): one slot note as the page shows it: its heading and key, `Stale` with the places that changed, its blocks and quotes, for a decision the human's why and source, and who reread it after its code moved and on what date. A note whose slot abbd does not know, whose file is not where its slot's note belongs, or whose entry its contract no longer lists, shows nowhere. `placeNotes` runs last in `Build`. A module's notes go in its own `Inside`, or under its entry in its parent's inside when it has none. A contract's go in the inside of its home: the module it sits in when that module provides it, else the provider whose provides relation, its own or one of a unit inside it, rests on the strongest origin, so a reader's coupling never moves a contract away from the provider the code shows. A unit no inside shows keeps its notes on its row of the unit list.
- **`Entries`** (`entries.go`): a contract's entries as one table, one `EntryRow` each with the entry's name and shape, its need, the units that provide it and those that use it from `topology.EntryUses`, and the reader's `entries` note on it. The shape column reads 시그니처, or 타입 for a contract that holds data, and the use columns read 쓰는 단위 and 읽는 단위 there. A column no row fills is left out. The page draws the table across the whole inside of the contract's home, below the drawing and the text, so nothing in it is cut. A header never breaks, an entry's name or shape keeps to one line up to 40 characters and wraps anywhere past that, so a long route or type with no space stays inside the page, and the description column takes the rest of the width and wraps. Each row's id is `e-` with the contract's ID and the entry's key, so two contracts with an entry of one key keep their own rows. A contract with entries shows in its home's inside even with no notes, and an entry's note shows on its row rather than among the contract's notes.
- **`site` / `Files`** (`page.go`, `page.html`, `page.css`): the embedded Korean template, one named template per kind of page (`index`, `module`, `around`, `flowpage`, `stackpage`, `tablepage`, `contracts`, `units`) around a shared `head` and `foot`, with its origin and kind labels and hover tips. The `css` func inlines `page.css` into every page, so each page opens on its own. A module's page shows its inside as `.inside.page`, and a focus view stays hidden until its anchor is the target, except on a page of one view, which wraps it in `.solo` to show it open. `Files` renders every page through `Pages` and adds `graph.json`, all from the model alone, so the same store renders the same bytes whatever sync wrote them.
- **`Pages`** (`site.go`): one `page` per file, each with its `Title`, its template and `Current`, the anchor of its own view (`overview`, `m-<ID>`, a focus view's or a figure's ID, `contracts` or `units`). A module page carries its `Inside` and its focus view on the overview as `Around`. A module page and a focus view's page carry a `Trail` of the modules from the top down to the page's unit, which the breadcrumbs and its nav draw. A figure's page belongs to no module, so its crumb names the figure. The overview lists each figure with counts only, of its STALE parts, its breaches and its unbacked and unsaid cells, so it changes only when a count does. Only the overview carries the `Nav` from `navTree`: each module that has a page, under the module that holds it, siblings by name. So a module that comes or goes rewrites the overview and its own page, and no other page. `TestAChangeInOneModuleRewritesOnlyItsPage` holds that a relation or a unit added inside a module rewrites only its page, `graph.json` and, for a unit, `units.html`, across the fixtures. The nav lists modules and features under separate headings, a top-level feature and everything under it counting as a feature, and marks the current page with `aria-current`. `TestTheNavListsFeaturesApartFromModules` holds that split on this repository's own state. `Paged` names the units that have a page, so a unit's name links there.
- **Indexes** (`site.go`, `page.html`): `contractRows` lists every contract grouped by kind, IF, EVT, DATA, then CFG, and by name within a kind. Each row names the module it sits in as its owner, and the units whose live relations provide and use it. Each is a `Ref` to the unit's page when it has one, else to its row `u-<ID>` in the unit index. `units.html` lists every unit by ID with its kind, its parent, its responsibility or purpose, its anchors, and its signature and quotes folded, so the browser's find reaches any ID, name, path or symbol.
- **`link`** (`site.go`): the template links by anchor, `href="#id"`, as if the site were one page. `link` reads every `id` each page holds and rewrites each anchor after all pages render. An anchor that is another page's own view opens that page at its top, an anchor the page itself holds stays as it is, and any other opens the first page in order that holds it, as `FILE#id`. An anchor no page holds stays unchanged. `site_test.go` checks that every link opens a page and a section that exist.
- **Figures** (`figure.go`, `flow.go`, `stack.go`, `table.go`): `flows` derives one `FlowView` per stored flow, `stacks` one `StackView` per stored stack and `tables` one `TableView` per stored table. `figureID` anchors a figure at `flow-KEY`, `stack-KEY` or `table-KEY`, and `partID` a part at `flow-KEY.N`, `stack-KEY.N` or `table-KEY.N`, which no key can produce. A part is STALE when `Part.Why` gives a reason, its code moved or the state lost what it names. A figure's badge is its weakest part origin, and in a mixed figure `partMarks` gives a part the human stated its own badge and source. In a flow, consecutive steps on one unit share a `Lane`, and a call shows only when `Step.Against` finds its relation. In a stack, each `Band` shows its layer's units as `UnitChip`s, and each breach `model.Breaches` finds sits in the band it leaves as a `BreachChip` that names both ends, the relation's verb and the higher layer. A lower unit that provides a higher layer's contract shows no chip. A stack's `Reasons` are the texts of `Figure.Gaps`, and so are a table's. In a table, each `ColumnHead` links the unit its column stands for, each `RowView` heads its row with a `UnitChip`, and each `CellView` takes the verb of the relation `model.Readings` finds under it and marks a cell `Unbacked` or `Unsaid`. `href` sends a click to the unit's own inside, else to the inside of the nearest unit holding it, else for a contract to its note home's inside. `linkFigures` lists on each inside the steps on the units it holds, the layers that hold its units and the rows its units head, by number. `page.html` draws a flow as a numbered list, a stack as a list of bands and a table as an HTML table, all of which wrap every line, and lists every figure by its question on the overview. At 560px and below a lane's unit and a band's name stand above their contents, and a table's rows fold to one card each, the row's unit across its top and every filled cell under its column's label.
- **Real states** (`fixtures_test.go`, `testdata/`): the unit files of four real stores, this repository, ripgrep, Orca and pstack, as sync left them. `TestEveryRealDrawingIsLegible` measures every full and focus view of each against `docs/blueprint-svg.md`'s rules. `debt` lists the views that do not keep them yet with what they measure now: a listed view may only improve, and the test fails once it keeps every rule and is still listed. `chips_test.go` measures a small hand-written drawing whose chips leave their box and the canvas, so the escaped-chip count cannot read 0 unnoticed.

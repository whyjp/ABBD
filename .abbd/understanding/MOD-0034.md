## Overview

`internal/screen` turns the `.abbd/` store into the blueprint a human reads. `Load` reads the store, `Build` derives a `Model`, and `Files` renders two files. `index.html` is one static page with no script. `graph.json` is the same data for tools. The CLI's `publish` (`cmd/abbd/screen.go`) saves them after every command marked `writes` (`setup`, `sync`, `record relation|unit|parent|fact|evidence`, `retract`, `migrate`, `understand`). `abbd status` reuses `Load` for its freshness verdicts. The package never writes the topology, and it draws no box the store lacks, apart from a focus view's twin.

The overview draws each top-level module and feature as a box. A blue line runs from a user's chip on the bottom edge to the provider's chip on the top edge. One chip stands for the contracts that line carries, named when it is one and counted when it is more, and the contracts no line reaches fold into one `+N 계약` chip on the provider, and a thick orange `bypass` line marks a module dependency no contract carries. Two modules that depend on each other that way share one `mutual` line with a head at each end. A relation from a unit at any depth is lifted to the top-level unit that contains it. INFERRED couplings are drawn too, because sync rechecks their quotes. Only dead ones are dropped, and a drifted one is drawn like a live one. A small circle at a line's start marks a reader's or a human's relation, and a dashed line runs against the reading direction. Every top-level module and every unit that holds others has its own view. An overview box links to its view only when `viewOf` names one, and `Build` makes a view for exactly the units `viewOf` names, so no link opens a missing section. A feature with no parts has none, so its box carries no link and its title offers no click. Everything is sorted before it is placed, so the same store always draws the same picture. `docs/blueprint-svg.md` states the drawing rules. `legible_test.go` measures them per line and channel, and `trace_test.go` traces every relation along the drawn ink for bends, crossings, phantom paths, unmarked splits and counts that belong to no single line.

## Key Concepts

- **`Input` / `Model`** (`screen.go`): `Input` is what `Load` reads. `Load` leaves `Tree`, the git snapshot, for the caller to fill. `Model` is what `Build` derives. It holds the unit catalogue, the `Overview`, `Links` between two contracts listed as text, one `Inside` per view, the overview's `Focus` views, every live relation as `[]Line` for `graph.json`, and both freshness values.
- **`tree`** (`tree.go`): the parent tree the views follow. A unit whose parent is absent moves to the top level, so nothing present drops out. A unit with children and no live relations of its own is abstract, and its box draws its members' relations (`sources`).
- **`overview`** (`overview.go`): the plan `planOverview` builds and the insides reuse. `top` lists the top-level units and the live relations between them. Each contract gets its `providers` and `users`, and each drawn edge keeps the stored relations it stands for and their origins.
- **`Drawing` / `Box` / `Port` / `Wire`** (`overview.go`): one SVG picture with its coordinates set. A `Box` is a top-level module or feature, an inside unit, or a contract no module provides. Its `Href` opens a view the page holds, and it stays empty when there is none. On the overview, `chipFor` makes one chip per set of contracts a drawn line carries, labelled with its one contract or with `계약 N개` joined by a no-break space so `shorten` keeps the word, and a module whose chips need more than `chipWrap` wraps them into at most two rows instead of widening, and the last row folds what does not fit into one "+N" `Port`. On an inside frame, two ports that land on one spot share a chip, and its `Keys` name every port it stands for. `Drawing.Relations` lists the pairs of places the view requested before routing, which the checker holds the ink to. `Wire.Kind` is the CSS class. `Origin`, `Marks`, `Back`, `Both` and `Count` carry the origin circle, the dash, a `mutual` line's second head and a bus's ×N.
- **`arrange` / `layered`** (`layout.go`, `layered.go`): the layered layout. It breaks cycles for ranking only and sets each node above what it uses. It then lays the rows out as a proper layered graph: each pair of places more than a row apart gets a `chain` of dummies, one per row it passes. `order` sorts every row on the whole graph, chains included, and `wrap` splits a layer wider than the width budget in that order. `place` treats each box and each chain as one block, Brandes and Köpf style, so a chain runs at one x and boxes step aside for it. `shed` then takes the shortest chains out while the rows, every chain crooked, are still wider than the budget, and renumbers the rest, so every chain left has its dummies. `place` keeps straight as many chains as fit the budget, found by halving, since each straight chain can only widen the drawing. `toward` tells the chips where their lines leave a row. A focus view passes `arrange` a `focusSeat`, the x of every box in its full view: `seat` then orders each row by that x instead of `order`, `brick` deals a ring too wide for one row into rows that each take every k-th box, and `ring` replaces `place`, setting the focus and the frame's lines into it on one vertical axis at the middle and packing each row around them.
- **`route`** (`route.go`): orthogonal routing on that grid. A line crosses each gap on its own track and passes rows down its chain, or between two boxes or beside the row where it has none. Lines of one kind that leave one box share a departure trunk through every row two of them still pass. Lines of one kind that reach one box share an arrival bus, and boxes side by side in one row share one only when exactly the same places reach them. Each trunk runs down the chain of its lead line, with its stub or drop right over it. A line that changes buses steps between them where no reader can follow other lines the wrong way: `viaX` keeps a step off a departure track between the two trunks, and `stepX` finds a step onto an arrival track the nearest x no other line uses. Lines that leave and reach one box edge share its slots. Each bus is drawn once with its line count. `place` then sizes each gap, with room beside each stub or drop that carries a count.
- **`junctions` / `anchor`** (`ink.go`): what the routed paths look like to a reader. `junctions` puts a `Dot` wherever ink of one colour goes three or more ways, and where a line crosses onto a trunk of its own kind that runs past it. `anchor` sets each ×N beside its own stub or drop, on the side where the nearest other ink is farthest.
- **`Inside`** (`inside.go`): one view built by `(*overview).inside`. Its frame carries the contracts of the module or feature it opens and dashed ports for the modules it meets without one. It also holds `Units` with one sentence each, `Unwired` contracts kept off the frame, `Inferred` couplings with their quotes, `Up` and `Trail`, and the `Understanding`.
- **`Focus`** (`focus.go`): a view derived from a dense one, which draws more than two relations per box. Each box of a dense overview or inside gets one: that box, the boxes it meets, and only its own lines, built by the same `planOverview` or `inside` with a focus ID. `planOverview` plans the whole overview first, and `around` then keeps only its lines with an end at the focus and the boxes at their other ends, so a focus view never draws a line the overview lacks; `TestFocusViewsArePartialGraphsOfTheirView` holds every focus view's lines, chips and boxes to its full view in both directions. A unit that both uses the focus and is used by it is drawn a second time below as a twin, ID `<unit>↓`, so every line reads down; `base` maps a twin back to its unit. Focus views take the overview's width, name boxes by their last path segment, link each ring box to its own focus view, and are left out of `graph.json`. `Render` gives a copy of each full view to `linkFocus`, which makes each box that opens nothing else open its focus view, so `graph.json` holds no link to a focus view, and the page lists the focus views under the full drawing. `centres` reads the full view's box centres for `focusSeat`, so a ring keeps the order its boxes have in the full view; `TestFocusViewsKeepTheMapOfTheirView` holds that order and the centred focus.
- **Freshness** (`screen.go`): `ActualFreshness` compares the working-tree snapshot with the synced fingerprint. `BlueprintFreshness` compares the rendered watermark with the inputs hash now.
- **`Understanding`** (`words.go`): a how text cut into `Block`s and `Span`s. It is marked `Stale` when the module's digest has moved. The template escapes it and never trusts its markup.
- **`page` / `Files`** (`page.go`): the embedded Korean template with its labels and tips. `Files` renders both files with the `inputs_hash` watermark.

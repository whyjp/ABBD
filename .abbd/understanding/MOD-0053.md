### Overview

`internal/model` is ABBD's vocabulary. It defines what a design unit is, how facts about it carry their provenance, which edges between units are legal, and what a cited piece of evidence looks like. It imports nothing from the rest of ABBD, and almost every other package imports it: `store` serializes its types as YAML, `extract` builds them from JSON, `evidence` checks their quotes, `topology` and `turn` compute over them.

Validation lives with the types. `Unit.Validate` enforces that the ID prefix matches the kind (`MOD-0001` is a `MOD`), that every unit has an anchor, that a why-field (purpose, responsibility) is only USER_STATED, ADOPTED or INFERRED, that only contracts carry a signature, that a parent is another module, and that a semantic unit has evidence.

### Key Concepts

- **`Fact`**: a value plus three independent axes. `Origin` says where it came from (USER_STATED, ADOPTED, EXTRACTED, OBSERVED, INFERRED), `Verification` whether it was checked, and `Confirmation` whether a human accepted or rejected it. `Guess` is true for an INFERRED fact that no human has decided.
- **`Kind`**: `MOD`, `IF`, `EVT`, `DATA`, `CFG`, `FEAT`. `Kind.Dir` maps each kind to `architecture` or `features`, and `IsContract` is true for IF/EVT/DATA/CFG. `DOM`, `STATE`, `TRN` and `INV` are reserved.
- **`ID`**: `KIND-NNNN` with at least four digits. `ParseID` and `FormatID` convert between the string and its parts.
- **`Unit`**: the stored record, with `Anchors`, `Parent`, `Evidence`, the three fact fields, `Digest`, outgoing `Relations`, `Absent`, and the user-owned `Notes`. `Semantic()` is true when the name's origin is INFERRED.
- **`Relation` and `RelationKind`**: `depends_on`, `consumes`, `provides`. The `ends` table and `Joins` allow only a module or feature to provide a contract, and allow anything to consume a contract or depend on any unit. `Counts` admits a reader-found edge into the actual topology only once a human confirms it, and drops it once `Dead`.
- **`Via`**: the closed list `import`, `trait`, `callback`, `macro`, `config`, `build-feature`, `data`, `convention`, `call`.
- **`Evidence`**: `Path`, optional `Lines` and `Symbol`, and `Quote`. `Span` parses `Lines`. `Validate` rejects paths under `.abbd/` or `.git/`. `ParseEvidence` reads the `PATH:LINES:QUOTE` form that `abbd propose` takes.

### Gotchas

- The via list and relation-kind rules are repeated as prose in the abbd-setup and abbd-mode skills. Change them in step.
- `Validate` duplicates the `.abbd` literal instead of importing `store.Dir`, because importing store would create a cycle.
- `ParseEvidence` splits on the first two colons, so a Windows drive path like `C:\...` does not parse. Paths must be relative.

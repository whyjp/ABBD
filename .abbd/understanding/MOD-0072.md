### Overview

`internal/topology` holds ABBD's central comparison: the intended topology a human approved (`Intended`, saved as `.abbd/intended.yaml`) against the actual topology extracted from code (`[]model.Unit`). `Compare` lists every difference as an `Item`. `Intended.Effect` gives each one the action it needs: `BLOCKED` (grade C), `REALIGN` or `PROMOTE`. `DerivePhase` turns the result into the project phase. Every other decision path calls into this package: `abbd status`, `align`, `approve`, the turn gate in `internal/turn` and the screen.

The package also owns every change to `Intended`. `Select` builds the first alignment from the units a human picks. `Approve` folds in one grade C item. `RealignTo` acknowledges the REALIGN rows a human chose, and refuses while a grade C item is pending. `Accept` is the unconditional fold a synced turn uses. `Adopt` promotes a below-the-line candidate. Each one returns a `normalized()` copy and never mutates in place.

### Key Concepts

- **Intended / IntendedUnit / Edge**: the approved units with kind and contract signature, plus the approved relations as `Edge{From, To, Kind}`. `Revision` counts the changes made to it.
- **Change and Item.Key**: change kinds such as `new-relation` and `contract-changed`, plus three turn-only findings (`Finding()`). `Item.Key` prints `change:ID` or `change:FROM.kind.TO`, and `ParseKey`/`ParseEdge` read those forms back. This is the `if-change-key` contract.
- **Effect rule**: anything that touches a contract's promise or its consumers and providers, or that joins two intended units, is `Blocked`. Other changes to intended units get `Realign`. A new unit gets `Promote`.
- **boundary**: a unit inside a design module stands for that module. A relation that stays inside the module is dropped. One that leaves it is graded as the module's own relation, so routing a coupling through an inside unit cannot hide it.
- **Phase**: `uninitialized` (no units), `bootstrapped` (no intended file), `aligned` (only PROMOTE items remain), `drifted`.
- **Couplings**: live, undecided semantic relations. They never gate, but the turn impact follows them and the screen draws them.
- **Candidates / PromotionQuestion**: below-the-line modules, contracts, and units that two or more design units relate to. The question lists the unit's relations, so a rejection holds only until they change.
- **HasConsumers**: whether a contract has a consumes or depends_on relation into it, or a human's `ExternalConsumers` flag.

### Gotchas

- The key and phase strings are repeated outside Go types. `internal/screen` builds `"new-relation:"+e.String()` by hand, `page.go` and `page.html` match the phase names and `"REALIGN"` as literals, and `cmd/abbd/serve.go` joins `from.kind.to` itself.
- `droppedWithUnit` hides a removed relation behind its unit's removal, except when the relation would gate harder, such as a deleted consumer of a contract.
- `Adopt` drops acknowledged relations whose grade rises once the unit becomes a design unit, so they are asked again.

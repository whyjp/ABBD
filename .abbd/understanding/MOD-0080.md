### Overview

`internal/turn` models one unit of agent work under abbd mode: what it may touch, what it says it will change, what a human allowed, and how it ended. The package is pure. It never reads or writes a file; `cmd/abbd/turn.go` loads the `Turn` from `.abbd/changes/CHG-*.md`, calls these functions, and saves the result through `internal/store`.

A turn runs in four stages. At start the CLI records a `Baseline` (tree fingerprint, unit `Digests`, `EvidenceDigests`) and the impact `ComputeImpact` derives from the seeds over `GraphOf`. At declare, `Declared` parses each change key with `topology.ParseKey` and grades it; a grade C key becomes a `Question`, capped at `MaxQuestions`. At end, `Judge` compares the actual topology with the intended one plus the turn's `Approvals`, and three grade B checks are added: `Touched`, `EvidenceTouched` and `Unscanned`. A turn can only end `SYNCED` when `Evidence` says its last `Run` passed on the current tree. Then `Fold` writes every grade B and approved grade C change into the intended topology.

### Key Concepts

- **Grade**: `A` proceeds silently, `B` proceeds and leaves an UNCONFIRMED ADR, `C` stops until a human approves it for this turn. `GradeOf` maps a `topology.Effect` to a grade; a signature change to a contract nobody consumes drops from C to B.
- **State**: `OPEN`, `BLOCKED`, `DRAFT`, `SYNCED`, `CLOSED`. `Live` turns take declarations; `Resumable` ones also take approvals.
- **Graph and Link**: the intended relations plus live semantic couplings from `topology.Couplings`. Following a guess never blocks.
- **Impact and Tiers**: `ComputeImpact` follows consumers, providers and provided contracts from the seeds. `Tiers` splits the recorded impact into `direct` and `far` each time it runs. `EnsureImpact` puts back computed items an agent removed.
- **Verdict**: one graded difference. `Verdict.String` prints the `A ...`, `B PROCEED`, `C BLOCKED` and `C APPROVED` lines the skill and the screen read.
- **Findings**: `contract-touched`, `evidence-touched` and `semantic-unscanned` are grade B notes for what the gate cannot read: a change of meaning under the same signature, edited lines under a confirmed coupling's quote, and a direct design unit that no `Scan` covered.
- **Question**: the text that identifies a human decision. It names the starting signature or the intended `Revision`, so an old answer never covers a new change.

### Gotchas

- The verdict line format is a contract nobody declared. `internal/screen` tokenizes `Report.Gate` lines and skips any line containing `PROMOTE`, and the abbd-mode skill quotes `B PROCEED` and `C BLOCKED`.
- `cmd/abbd` writes ADR grades as the literals `"B"` and `"C"`, not `turn.B`/`turn.C`.
- `Scanners` (`how`, `blast-radius`) must match the `--by` values the abbd-mode skill tells agents to pass.
- `Evidence` assumes `Run.Output` is relative to `.abbd/`, which only the CLI's `verification/runs/` path ensures.

## Overview

`internal/model` is ABBD's vocabulary. It holds the Go types for everything stored under `.abbd/architecture/` and `.abbd/features/`, the rules that decide which values are legal, and the table that decides which writer's claim shows. It imports only the standard library. `model.go` holds origins, kinds, IDs, units, facts, evidence, relations and edges. `claims.go` holds writers, evidence status, the precedence table, parent claims, masks and `Resolve`. `figure.go` holds what every kind of figure shares, and `flow.go` and `layer.go` hold the two kinds, a flow's steps and a stack's layers, with the stack's rules for holding units and for breaches. Every other internal package but `gitx` and `narrative` imports it (`bootstrap`, `evidence`, `extract`, `group`, `identity`, `screen`, `store`, `topology`), and so does `cmd/abbd`. It does no I/O, runs no git and renders nothing.

The package exists to keep provenance honest. Every fact, relation and parent claim stores the `Writer` that made it, and its origin follows from the writer and is never stored. `script` and `group` are EXTRACTED, `supplement` and `reader` are INFERRED, and `human` is USER_STATED. A value with an unknown or empty writer fails validation, so no empty value can mean EXTRACTED. `Unit.Validate` enforces the consequences. A human's fact, parent, relation or mask needs a `Source`, and no other writer may carry one. A why-field is never the script's or the group's. A unit whose name a reader or the supplement wrote needs evidence, and so does an INFERRED parent or relation, while an EXTRACTED parent cites none. Only contracts carry a `Signature`, a parent must be another module or feature, and a unit holds at most one parent claim per writer and one relation claim per writer, kind and end. An absent unit needs a known reason, and a present one has none. `RelationKind.Joins` checks that an edge joins legal kinds. `ParseEvidence` and `ParseEdge` read what `abbd record` and `abbd retract` get on the command line. `Resolve` derives the read model every other package uses. Downstream code branches on small predicates. `Relation.Counts` is true for an EXTRACTED or USER_STATED claim that is not dead, and only `topology.Compare` calls it, to decide which relations between modules `abbd diff --check` sees. Its comment says a reader's coupling is listed instead of drawn, but `internal/screen` draws INFERRED edges that are not dead too. `Present` drops absent units and sorts by ID.

## Key Concepts

- **`Unit`** (`model.go`): one design unit. It has an `ID`, a `Kind`, a `Name` fact and at least one `Anchor`. It may have a `SupplementRef`, stored as `supplement_ref`, the `Parents` claims of each writer, its own `Evidence`, the why-fields `Purpose` and `Responsibility`, a contract `Signature`, `Entries` and `Digest`, the shown `Relations`, the `Hidden` claims, `Masks`, and `Absent` with an `AbsentReason` (`not-reported`, `withdrawn`, `evidence-dead`, `retracted` or `unneeded`). The derived `Parent`, `ParentOrigin` and `ParentWriter` and the user-owned `Notes` stay out of the YAML. `SetAbsent` sets or clears both absence fields. `Claims` lists shown and hidden relation claims together. `EachEvidence` visits every stored item with its claim's writer, and `EachShownEvidence` only the unit's own, the winning parent's and the shown relations' items. `ShownParent` returns the parent claim that won. `Semantic` reports an INFERRED name.
- **`Kind`** (`model.go`): `MOD`, the contract kinds `IF`, `EVT`, `DATA` and `CFG` (`IsContract`), and `FEAT`. `Dir()` sends features to `features` and the rest to `architecture`. `Validate` rejects the reserved `DOM`, `STATE`, `TRN` and `INV` and any unknown kind. `Holds()` is true for `MOD` and `FEAT`, the two kinds that may contain others.
- **`ID`** (`model.go`): `KIND-NNNN`, such as `MOD-0007`. `FormatID` pads to four digits and `ParseID` accepts four or more.
- **`Fact`** (`model.go`): a `Value`, its `Writer`, and a `Source` for a human only. `Origin` is derived by `Writer.Origin()` (`claims.go`), which is empty for a writer ABBD does not know. `Said` and `Stated` build one, and `Guess` reports an INFERRED value.
- **`Anchor`** (`model.go`): the `Path` and optional `Symbol` a unit's identity hangs on. `Key()` is `path#symbol`, and `SortAnchors` dedups and sorts by it. `Closeness` says how closely the anchor names the code a quote lies in, or -1 when it does not hold it: a symbol anchor holds only a quote that names its symbol in its file and is closest, then a file anchor, then a directory anchor, deeper first, and `.` holds every file least closely. `Evidence.Anchor` turns an item into one.
- **`Evidence`** (`model.go`): a verbatim `Quote` at a `Path`, with optional `Lines` and `Symbol`, and the `Status`, `Since` and `Reason` that sync stores and the JSON never sets. A status is live (empty), `drifted` or `dead`, and a dead item needs a reason. `Same` compares path and quote only. `Span` parses `Lines`, and `Validate` accepts only a range such as `120` or `120-134` that starts at 1 or later. `Validate` also refuses paths under `.abbd/` or `.git/`, because those files repeat the quote. `StatusOf` (`claims.go`) makes a claim live while any item is live, else drifted while any is drifted, else dead, and a claim with no items is live.
- **`Slot` and `NoteRef`** (`slot.go`): `Slots` is the one list of slots a module or a contract takes (`Kind.HoldsNotes`), in the order the blueprint shows them, each with its Korean heading. `decisions` and `entries` are keyed, so each holds one note per key. A note in `entries` describes one entry of a contract, and `NoteRef.Unheld` reports one whose key names no entry the unit lists. `ParseNoteRef` reads `ID.SLOT` or `ID.decisions.KEY` and refuses an unknown slot, a keyed slot without a key, a key on any other slot, and a key outside lower-case letters, digits and hyphens.
- **`Relation`** (`model.go`): one writer's claim on an edge from the unit that holds it, with `To`, `Kind`, `Writer`, `Source`, `Via`, `Evidence`, `Entries` and `Digest`. `Entries` narrows the claim to those entries of the contract it points at, by key: the routes a page calls, the fields a module writes. `Origin` and `Dead` are derived, and `Dead` means it has evidence and all of it is dead. `Status`, `Drifted`, `Semantic` (it has evidence), `Guess` (it is INFERRED) and `Counts` read it. `SortRelations` orders claims by end and then kind.
- **`Entry`** (`entry.go`): one part of a contract as the code spells it, such as a route, a method, a flag or a field. `Name` is the code's spelling and `Key` that name reduced by `EntryKey` to lower-case letters, digits and hyphens, so `GET /users/{id}` is `get-users-id` and a note on it is `ID.entries.get-users-id`. `Shape` is what the code says the entry looks like, and `Required` is nil when the code does not say. `Need` words it as required, optional or nothing, and `Look` joins shape and need for a diff line. `ValidateEntries` refuses an entry without a name, an entry whose name reduces to no key, such as one in Hangul, with a word to give it a `key`, a malformed key and two entries with one key, and `Unit.Validate` refuses entries on a unit that is not a contract. `EntryNamed` finds one by key.
- **Relation grammar** (`model.go`): `depends_on` joins any two kinds, `consumes` ends at a contract, and `provides` runs from a module or feature to a contract. `Via` is empty or one of `import`, `trait`, `callback`, `macro`, `config`, `build-feature`, `data`, `convention` or `call`.
- **`Edge`** (`model.go`): `From`, `Kind` and `To`, printed as the shell-safe `MOD-0001.depends_on.MOD-0002` and read back by `ParseEdge`. `Edge.Joins` checks the kinds its IDs name.
- **`Precedence`** (`claims.go`): one table of the writers that may claim each field, highest first. Name and signature take script, reader, supplement. Purpose and responsibility take human, reader, supplement. A relation takes script, human, reader, supplement, and a parent takes human, group, script, reader, supplement. `Outranks` compares two writers on a field, and a writer missing from a row ranks below all of it. On a relation or a parent, `order` puts live claims before drifted ones before dead ones, whatever the writer, and the table decides within a status.
- **`ParentClaim`** (`claims.go`): one writer's claim on the unit's container, with `To`, `Writer`, `Source` and `Evidence`. A human's claim also stores `Over`, the script's parent when the human stated it, and `ParentConflicts` lists each present unit whose script parent has moved off it to a parent other than the human's. `Claim` reads a writer's claim, `PutParent` replaces it and keeps the claims in table order, and `DropParents` removes the claims of the given writers.
- **`Mask`** (`claims.go`): a reader's or a human's retraction of one `Target` (`unit`, `parent`, `purpose`, `responsibility`, `signature`, or `KIND.TO` from `RelationTarget`), with a `Reason`. It is bound to the hidden claim's content by `Digest`, the first 16 hex digits of a SHA-256. `RelationDigest` hashes kind, end and via, `ParentDigest` the parent, and `FactDigest` the value. `Hides` lets a reader's mask hide the supplement's claims and a human's hide the reader's and the supplement's. `Masked` tests a claim, and `PutMask` adds a mask only once. A claim whose content changes no longer matches and shows again.
- **`Resolve`** (`claims.go`): derives the read model in place. A masked why-field or signature is cleared, and a present unit whose name is masked goes absent as `retracted`. On each edge the claims sort by `order` with masked claims last, the first shows unless it is masked, and the rest go to `Hidden`. `PutRelation` and `DropRelations` re-run this per unit after each edit. Each present unit takes its first parent claim that is not dead, not masked, points at a present module or feature and closes no cycle. Claims are accepted tier by tier, script and group first, then human, reader and supplement, each tier in unit ID order. When a claim is refused for closing a cycle, acceptance starts again from the first tier, so the unit's next claim is weighed before any lower tier. An absent unit shows its first stored claim. A skipped claim stays stored. `ParentCycle` names a present unit whose parents loop, and the tests of `model`, `identity` and `group` use it to check that the result is a tree.
- **Figures** (`figure.go`): what every kind of figure shares. A `FigureKind[C]` is one kind as data. `Noun` and `Dir` name a figure and its directory under `.abbd`, `Part` and `Parts` name one claim and the frontmatter key that lists them, `Min` and `Max` bound how many a figure has, and `Whole` checks the claims taken together, nil when each stands alone. `Claim[C]` is what a claim type implements so that the store and the command treat every kind alike: its `Kind`, read on the zero value by `KindOf[C]`, `Validate`, a `Label` for status, the units it `Names`, its one-line `Words` in a fixed order, its `Quotes`, `Requote`, and its `Gaps` against the state. `FigureKey` and `ParseFigureKey[C]` take the same keys a decision does. `state` holds the present units by ID, `under` walks a unit's parent chain, and `rests` holds the closest-anchor rule for a set of units. One quote at least must lie in code that one of them, or a unit inside one, anchors more closely than any other unit does, or that a contract one of them provides anchors so. So a shared file counts for the unit whose anchor names it best, a command's handler counts for the module that provides the command, and a unit a step calls lends it no code.
- **`Step`** (`flow.go`): one step of a flow, the kind `flow` whose parts are `steps`, two to `FlowSteps`, 16. It has a unit, a one-line `Does`, `Note` and `Fails`, the units it `Calls`, and the evidence it rests on. `Validate` checks a step alone. `Against` reads it against the state. It finds a gap for a unit that is gone and a gap when no quote rests on the step's unit by `rests`. For each call it finds the strongest live relation, provides then consumes then depends_on, from the step's unit or a unit inside it to the called unit or a unit inside that, or a gap when none joins them. So a flow's call never stands for an edge the graph lacks. A step's `Gaps` are those of `Against`.
- **`Layer`** (`layer.go`): one band of a stack, the kind `stack` whose parts are `layers`, two to `StackLayers`, 8. It has a one-line `Name` and `Role`, the stored `Units` it holds, each with every unit inside it, and the evidence that shows its role. `Validate` checks a layer alone, and `Gaps` finds a unit that is gone, or no quote resting on its units by `rests`. The kind's `Whole` is `holdOnce`. A unit sits in one layer only, counting the units inside the ones a layer names, and the layers name `StackUnits`, 16, at most. `Holders` maps each present unit to the layer that names it or its nearest named ancestor. `Breaches` lists each live relation of a present unit, of any kind and in its stored direction, that runs from a lower layer up to a higher one, with its kind, via and origin. A relation within one layer, one that skips layers downward, and one with an end no layer holds are no breach.

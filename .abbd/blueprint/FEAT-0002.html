    Overview

    The stance at the top of the /abbd-setup skill: setup asks the human one thing only, how dense the overview should be, and only when a human is in the conversation. The first state it commits is the baseline, which the human reads in the blueprint and the PR, and setup needs nothing but the abbd CLI and the reader, with pstack as an optional engine.
